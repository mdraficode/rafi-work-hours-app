Log a working day

Sequence: Date → Tour package → Work hours. Work hours are pre-filled with the package default — Sundays and Finnish public holidays are doubled automatically ❄ — but every field stays editable.

h
Which days count as double (❄)?
  • All Sundays
  • New Year's Day (1.1) · Epiphany (6.1)
  • Good Friday · Easter Sunday (moveable — Easter 2026: 5.4)
  • May Day (1.5)
  • Midsummer's Eve (Friday) & Midsummer's Day (Saturday) — midsummer 2026: 19.–20.6
  • All Saints' Day (Saturday between 31.10 and 6.11) — 2026: 31.10
  • Independence Day (6.12)
  • Christmas Eve (24.12) · Christmas Day (25.12) · Second Day of Christmas (26.12)

Moveable dates are calculated automatically for any year. Easter Monday, Ascension and Pentecost are not Finnish public holidays, so they are not doubled — if your employer pays them double, just type the hours in manually.
